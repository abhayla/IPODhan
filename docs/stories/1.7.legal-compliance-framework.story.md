## Acceptance Criteria

**1. SEBI Compliance Requirements:**
- Mandatory disclaimer on every page: "Investment in securities market are subject to market risks. Read all the related documents carefully before investing"
- Secondary disclaimer: "IPODhan is not a SEBI registered investment advisor. Information is for educational purposes only"
- Disclaimer placement: Top of page (banner) and footer (persistent)
- Font size: Minimum 12px, high contrast for visibility
- Additional IPO-specific disclaimers on relevant pages

**2. Terms of Service Document:**
- Comprehensive terms covering:
  - Service description and limitations
  - User obligations and age requirements (18+)
  - Disclaimer of warranties
  - Limitation of liability
  - Intellectual property rights
  - Third-party links and affiliate relationships
  - Indemnification clauses
  - Dispute resolution (Mumbai jurisdiction)
  - Modification rights
- Versioning system with date stamps
- User acceptance tracking (when registration is added)

**3. Privacy Policy Implementation:**
- Compliant with India's Data Protection Bill 2023
- Sections covering:
  - Information collected (automatic for MVP, personal data later)
  - How information is used
  - Data sharing policies
  - Security measures
  - User rights (access, correction, deletion)
  - Cookie policy
  - Contact information
- Regular review and update schedule

**4. Cookie Consent Implementation:**
- Banner appears on first visit
- Granular consent options:
  - Necessary cookies (required)
  - Analytics cookies (optional)
  - Marketing/Affiliate cookies (optional)
  - Functional cookies (optional)
- "Accept All" and "Accept Selected" options
- Link to detailed cookie policy
- Consent stored in localStorage
- Re-consent on policy changes

**5. Affiliate Disclosure:**
- Clear disclosure on broker comparison pages
- Format: "IPODhan may earn commission when you open demat accounts through our partner links"
- Separate detailed affiliate disclosure page
- List of all affiliate partners with SEBI registration numbers
- Statement that affiliate relationships don't influence scores/recommendations

**6. Investment Advisor Disclaimer:**
- Prominent statement: "IPODhan is NOT a SEBI-registered investment advisor"
- Clear explanation that content is educational/informational only
- Not registered under:
  - SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013
  - SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014
  - SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020
- Advice to consult qualified financial advisors

**7. Grey Market Premium (GMP) Disclaimer:**
- Warning box on all GMP displays
- Key points:
  - GMP is unofficial and unregulated
  - Can be volatile and change rapidly
  - Trading in grey market is illegal in India
  - For informational purposes only
  - Should not be sole basis for investment decisions

**8. Data Accuracy Disclaimer:**
- Sources disclosure (NSE, BSE, SEBI, RHP/DRHP)
- Update frequency disclosure
- "Information provided as-is" statement
- No guarantee of accuracy or completeness
- Encouragement to verify with official sources

**9. Legal Pages Structure:**
- Dedicated routes for each legal document
- SEO optimized with appropriate meta tags
- Easy navigation from footer
- Mobile-responsive design
- Print-friendly versions

**10. Footer Legal Section:**
- Persistent SEBI disclaimer
- Links to all legal documents
- Copyright notice
- Regulatory status clarification

**11. Compliance Monitoring:**
- Track disclaimer views
- Log consent actions
- Audit trail for compliance
- Regular compliance reports
- Page compliance verification system

**12. Legal Review Process:**
- Initial review by legal counsel
- Quarterly review schedule
- Update process for regulatory changes
- Version control for all legal documents

```
│   ├── terms/

│   ├── privacy/

│   ├── disclaimer/
│   │   └── page.tsx                      # General Disclaimer page
│   ├── cookie-policy/
│   │   └── page.tsx                      # Cookie Policy page
│   ├── affiliate-disclosure/
│   │   └── page.tsx                      # Affiliate Disclosure page
│   └── layout.tsx                        # Add SEBI banner + footer
│
├── stores/
│   └── consent-store.ts                  # Cookie consent state (Zustand)
│
└── lib/
    ├── compliance/

    │   └── validator.ts                  # Page compliance validator
    └── constants/
        └── legal-content.ts              # Legal text content
```

### Data Flow
1. **Initial Page Load:**
   - Check localStorage for cookie consent
   - Show cookie banner if no consent found
   - Display SEBI disclaimer banner on all pages
   - Render legal footer with links

2. **Cookie Consent Flow:**
   - User selects cookie preferences
   - Store consent in localStorage
   - Initialize analytics based on consent
   - Track consent event for compliance audit

3. **Legal Page Access:**
   - Route to /terms, /privacy, etc.
   - Load legal content from constants
   - Track page view for compliance
   - Display version and last updated date

4. **Compliance Tracking:**
   - Log all disclaimer impressions
   - Track consent actions
   - Generate compliance reports
   - Verify all pages have required disclaimers

### Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **UI:** React components with Tailwind CSS
- **State:** Zustand for consent management
- **Storage:** localStorage for consent persistence
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics (if user consented)
- **SEO:** next/head for meta tags

## Risk Assessment

### High Risks
1. **Legal Content Accuracy** - Legal disclaimers must be reviewed by legal counsel before launch
   - **Mitigation:** Get legal review from qualified attorney familiar with SEBI regulations

2. **Compliance Gaps** - Missing disclaimers on any page could lead to regulatory issues
   - **Mitigation:** Automated compliance validator checks all pages for required disclaimers

### Medium Risks
1. **Cookie Consent Edge Cases** - Users may clear localStorage, leading to re-consent
   - **Mitigation:** Handle re-consent gracefully, don't block critical functionality

2. **SEO Impact** - Legal banners may affect page layout and SEO
   - **Mitigation:** Use semantic HTML and proper ARIA labels, keep banners minimal

### Low Risks
1. **Performance Impact** - Additional components may increase bundle size
   - **Mitigation:** Code split legal pages, lazy load consent banner

## Notes

### Legal Review Required
This story creates the technical implementation of legal pages and disclaimers. **Legal content must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel** before production deployment. For MVP, we use placeholder content that follows SEBI guidelines, but final approval is required.

### Compliance Tracking
The compliance tracking system (Task 7) provides audit trail capabilities. In MVP, events are stored in localStorage. Future enhancement: send compliance events to backend API for centralized tracking and reporting.

### Affiliate Disclosure Integration
The AffiliateDisclosure component is created in this story but will be integrated into broker comparison pages when those pages are built (likely Story 1.8 or later).

### Cookie Consent vs Analytics
Cookie consent is implemented in this story. Google Analytics integration is covered in Story 1.8 (Basic Analytics & Monitoring). The consent store created here will be used by Story 1.8 to conditionally initialize analytics.
