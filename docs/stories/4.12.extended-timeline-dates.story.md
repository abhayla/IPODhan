# Story 4.12: Extended Timeline Dates

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** IPO applicant tracking my investment,
**I want** to see extended timeline dates (Basis of Allotment, Initiation of Refunds, Credit of Shares),
**so that** I can track the complete post-closure process and know when to expect allotment status, refunds, or shares in my demat account.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Add Basis of Allotment Date to IPO Detail Timeline**
   - Display `basis_of_allotment_date` in IPO Detail page timeline
   - Label: "Basis of Allotment"
   - Position: After "Allotment Date", before "Listing Date"
   - Format: "MMM DD, YYYY"

2. **Add Initiation of Refunds Date to Timeline**
   - Display `initiation_of_refunds_date` in timeline
   - Label: "Refunds Initiated"
   - Position: After "Basis of Allotment"
   - Show only for closed/listed IPOs

3. **Add Credit of Shares Date to Timeline**
   - Display `credit_of_shares_date` in timeline
   - Label: "Shares Credited"
   - Position: After "Refunds Initiated", before "Listing Date"
   - Important for investors to know when shares appear in demat

4. **Calendar Integration**
   - Add all 3 dates to Mainboard/SME IPO Calendar views
   - Display as calendar events with distinct colors
   - Clicking event shows IPO details

5. **Countdown/Elapsed Time Indicators**
   - Show countdown for upcoming dates
   - Show elapsed time for past dates
   - Format: "In 3 days" or "2 days ago"
   - Only show for relevant dates (not for upcoming IPOs)

6. **Null Handling**
   - Hide dates that are null
   - Show "TBD" if IPO is open/closed but dates not yet announced
   - No errors for missing dates
