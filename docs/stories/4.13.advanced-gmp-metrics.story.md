# Story 4.13: Advanced GMP Metrics

## Status
Draft

## Story

**As a** grey market participant or sophisticated IPO investor,
**I want** to see advanced GMP metrics (Kostak Rate, Subject Rate, Sauda Details),
**so that** I can understand the complete grey market trading picture and make informed decisions about IPO applications.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Display Kostak Rate**
   - Show `kostak_rate` in GMP Tab
   - Label: "Kostak Rate: ₹X"
   - Tooltip: "Rate at which allotment rights are traded in grey market (without shares)"
   - Position: Below main GMP value
   - Show "N/A" if not available

2. **Display Subject Rate**
   - Show `subject_rate` in GMP Tab
   - Label: "Subject/Safalya Rate: ₹X"
   - Tooltip: "Rate for trading assuming allotment (subject to getting shares)"
   - Position: Below Kostak Rate
   - Show "N/A" if not available

3. **Display Sauda Details**
   - Show `sauda_details` text in expandable section
   - Label: "Grey Market Trading Details"
   - Collapsible/expandable card
   - Format text with line breaks for readability
   - Hide if sauda_details is null/empty

4. **Educational Tooltips**
   - Add "What is grey market?" info icon
   - Explain Kostak vs Subject rate difference
   - Disclaimer about unofficial/unregulated nature

5. **Null Handling**
   - Show main GMP even if advanced metrics are null
   - Only show advanced metrics that have data
   - No errors for missing fields

## Dev Notes

### Database Context
[Source: screen-table-database-field-mapping.md#advanced-gmp-metrics]

**gmp_records Table Advanced Fields:**
- `kostak_rate` (INTEGER) - Allotment rights trading rate
- `subject_rate` (INTEGER) - Subject to allotment rate
- `sauda_details` (TEXT) - Grey market trading information

**Definitions:**
- **Kostak:** Trading allotment rights without shares (₹X per lot)
- **Subject/Safalya:** Trading assuming you get allotment (₹X per share)
- **Sauda:** General grey market trading details and conditions
