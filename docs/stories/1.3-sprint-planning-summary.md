## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: External Data Dependencies (MEDIUM)
**Issue:** Social buzz & search trends require external APIs (Twitter, Google Trends)
**Mitigation:** MVP approach - skip both components, recalculate weights proportionally
**Impact:** Reduces sentiment score accuracy by ~15%, but still functional
**Owner:** Product Owner (decision made: use MVP approach v1.1)

### Risk 2: Industry PE Benchmark Availability (LOW)
**Issue:** NSE sector PE data may not be easily accessible
**Mitigation:** Use hardcoded sector averages as fallback (Tech: 35, BFSI: 20, etc.)
**Impact:** Slightly less accurate fundamentals scoring, but acceptable
**Owner:** Developer (implement fallback logic)

### Risk 3: Performance Requirements (MEDIUM)
**Issue:** Score calculation <2s per IPO may be challenging with complex logic
**Mitigation:** Implement Redis caching, optimize database queries, batch processing
**Impact:** May require performance tuning iteration
**Owner:** Developer (Task 8 includes performance monitoring)

### Risk 4: Prediction Accuracy Target (MEDIUM)
**Issue:** >70% correlation with listing gains is ambitious for initial algorithm
**Mitigation:** Continuous improvement via A/B testing framework (AC7)
**Impact:** May take 3 months post-launch to reach target
**Owner:** Product team (monitor weekly accuracy reports)

### Risk 5: Learning Curve (LOW-MEDIUM)
**Issue:** Developer may be unfamiliar with FastAPI, Redis, or scoring algorithms
**Mitigation:** Exceptional documentation (742 lines), code examples, Story 1.2 patterns
**Impact:** May extend timeline by 2-3 days
**Owner:** Developer (allocated 8-10 days vs 5-7 days baseline)
