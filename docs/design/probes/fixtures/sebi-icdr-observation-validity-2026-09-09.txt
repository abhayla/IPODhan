SEBI ICDR observation-letter validity — the clause the lapsed-draft rule (OD-35) rests on
Fetched: 2026-09-09 (IST) by the pull-model delta run. Saved because a design rule that says
"twelve months" must cite the regulation, not a memory of it.

WHAT WAS ASKED
--------------
How long does an issuer have to OPEN a public issue after SEBI issues its observations on the draft
offer document? The design's lapsed-draft rule uses that period to decide whether an old draft with
no open date is a lapsed offering (new row) or a re-filing of the same one (same row).

SOURCE 1 — the regulation, quoted by Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas & Co
--------------------------------------------------------------------
URL: https://www.amsshardul.com/insight/relaxation-of-compliances-under-icdr-regulations-re-validity-of-sebi-observations-rights-issues/
Fetched: 2026-09-09

  "A public issue/rights issue may be opened within twelve months from the date of issuance of
   observations by SEBI, in terms of Regulation 44(1), 85 and 140."

=> TWELVE months, SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018,
   Regulation 44(1) for a public issue (85 and 140 carry the same period for the other issue types).

SOURCE 2 — the eighteen-month variant, and a live relaxation (secondary, NOT verbatim-verified)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Commentary on SEBI's circular of 2026-04-07 (taxmann.com, mondaq.com, studycafe.in; searched
2026-09-09) states two further things which the design must not ignore:

  (a) Regulations 44(1) AND 59C are described together as requiring a public issue to be opened
      "within twelve months or eighteen months from the date of issuance of SEBI's observations" —
      i.e. an eighteen-month period exists for a class of issues. The verbatim text of 59C was NOT
      reachable in this run, so the design treats eighteen months as REPORTED, not verified.
  (b) SEBI granted a ONE-TIME RELAXATION on 2026-04-07: observation letters expiring between
      2026-04-01 and 2026-09-30 are valid until 2026-09-30, subject to a Lead Manager undertaking
      under Schedule XVI.

WHAT THE DESIGN DOES WITH THIS
------------------------------
1. The lapsed-draft threshold is TWELVE months, citing Regulation 44(1).
2. Because an eighteen-month class exists and one-time relaxations are issued (2026-04-07 is one),
   the threshold is CONFIGURATION, not a constant in code (OD-51), and the rule is written so that
   changing it is a config edit with a dated reason — never a code deploy.
3. A draft that is past the threshold is NOT declared lapsed on the calendar alone: the row is
   frozen with a forward-linking notice only when the draft is past the period AND no RHP has
   arrived. A relaxation that we did not notice therefore costs a stale notice, never a deleted row.
